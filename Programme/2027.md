## English

### Sky Design Awards 2027 — Call for Entries

Sky Design Awards by Merci Magazine recognises architecture, interior design, product, graphic and branding projects. Founded in Japan in 2019, the programme is judged by leaders from design, hospitality and property development, so that every entry is assessed for its design and for its real value to business.

#### Categories

Architecture, Interior Design, Products, Graphics, Branding

#### Entry fees and deadlines

| | USD | |
|---|---|---|
| Early Bird | 370 | Now – 30 November 2026 |
| Regular | 420 | 1 – 31 December 2026 |
| Late | 499 | 1 – 15 January 2027 |

Pay now, send materials later. Your fee is set by the date you submit the application form and payment. If you pay within Early Bird, you keep the Early Bird fee and may send or complete your project materials until 15 January 2027.

#### Special Prizes

- **Stellar Works Special Prize** (Continues in 2027): Selected by Daisuke Hironaka, CEO of Stellar Works. With Stellar Works' support and sponsorship, the selected designer develops a furniture prototype.
- **LASVIT Japan Special Prize** (New for 2027): LASVIT Japan joins as sponsor and prize strategic partner. The prize is a prototype exploring glass and lighting design, made with LASVIT's craftsmanship.
