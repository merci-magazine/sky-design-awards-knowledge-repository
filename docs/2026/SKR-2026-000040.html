Anicom Holdings New Hub Project — Phase 1

SKR-2026-000040 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Anicom Holdings New Hub Project — Phase 1, designed by SPACE CO., LTD. / Daichi Hayashihara, located in Tokyo, Japan. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

SPACE CO., LTD. is designing the Anicom Holdings New Hub in Tokyo, which merges advanced veterinary diagnostics and human dental care within a single, transparent clinical facility. This project establishes a scalable architectural model for delivering pet-inclusive healthcare.

Editorial

In traditional healthcare, the paths of veterinary and human medicine often diverge, each operating within distinct clinical protocols and systems. Veterinary practices have developed independently, just as human medicine has, creating separate domains for patient care, equipment, and treatment environments. However, Anicom Holdings’ innovative new hub in Tokyo challenges this longstanding separation by integrating advanced veterinary services with a human dental clinic, thereby revolutionizing our approach to holistic wellbeing.

Designed by SPACE CO., LTD., this ambitious project reflects Anicom Holdings’ vision of health as a shared concern for both humans and animals. The first completed phase successfully connects the JARVIS Animal Medical Center Tokyo with the Anicom Healthy Life Extension Foundation's dental clinic. Future expansions plan to incorporate research, education, and corporate functions, paving the way for a comprehensive health institution.

The significance of co-locating these services transcends clinical efficacy; it addresses a profound social need. As companion animals increasingly take center stage in family dynamics, their presence influences emotional wellbeing and healthcare decisions. By allowing patients to attend dental appointments alongside their pets, the facility not only acknowledges the bond between humans and animals but also marks a significant shift in how healthcare environments can adapt to contemporary lifestyles.

JARVIS sets a high bar for veterinary care, equipped with cutting-edge technology including MRI, CT, endoscopy, and robotic-assisted surgery, complemented by intensive care and 24/7 emergency services. The architectural design must seamlessly incorporate this advanced technology, ensuring infection control while facilitating efficient clinical workflows, all in a setting that remains welcoming to pet owners during vulnerable times. SPACE CO., LTD. has crafted a cohesive architectural language that harmonizes these diverse care modalities. A palette of pale surfaces, metal, glass, and continuous lighting fosters visual continuity across the lower levels. Each service area retains its identity through differentiated access and clinical zoning, allowing the building to function as a singular institution without sacrificing the unique needs of veterinary and human healthcare.

Transparency is a foundational principle in this design. Treatment and imaging areas are strategically located to be visible from common circulation spaces, making advanced medical technologies and specialized veterinary care integral to the experience. While technology enhances the treatment environment, it does so without overwhelming visitors; rather, it makes expert capabilities tangible and accessible. This transparency is particularly crucial in veterinary settings, where pets cannot express their ailments, often heightening their owners’ anxiety. By demystifying diagnostic and treatment processes through thoughtful design, the hub fosters a reassuring atmosphere for both animals and their caregivers, while ensuring privacy for sensitive procedures.

Lighting further reinforces the emotional and functional narrative of the space. Clinical areas are brightly illuminated for precise treatment and imaging, while reception and waiting areas cultivate a warmer, quieter ambiance. Green lighting accents certain medical zones, creating a distinctive visual identity, while at night, the building becomes a captivating feature in Tokyo’s urban landscape. The serene aesthetic is meticulously supported by rigorous technical specifications, including antibacterial and antiviral finishes, durable surfaces, recycled flooring, and carefully delineated circulation paths, all of which contribute to a safe and comfortable environment for diverse types of care. Looking ahead, the project is designed with scalability in mind. Above the clinical floors, future plans include dedicated spaces for research, education, and corporate functions, enabling the building to evolve into a comprehensive platform for knowledge, treatment, and collaboration. Thus, the hub begins as a dynamic medical institution while retaining flexibility to expand Anicom’s ecosystem in the future.

Ultimately, the Anicom Holdings New Hub embodies a reimagined healthcare environment where human dentistry and advanced veterinary medicine coexist, sharing an address and an institutional identity, while fostering a deeper understanding of collective wellbeing. SPACE CO., LTD. has established a spatial framework that emphasizes clarity, visibility, and meticulously managed clinical infrastructure.

This innovative project illustrates a modern interpretation of care, where the health of both people and animals is increasingly recognized as interconnected and essential to daily living.
