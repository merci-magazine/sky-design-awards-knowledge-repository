MITAMACHI TERRACE CONFERENCE and SENQ TAMACHI

SKR-2026-000007 is the Sky Knowledge Record for MITAMACHI TERRACE CONFERENCE and SENQ TAMACHI, designed by KOKUYO Co., Ltd., located in Tamachi–Mita, Tokyo, Japan. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

At MITAMACHI TERRACE, a pale-blue workers’ lounge and a warm, grounded SENQ hall turn the language of stations and boat landings into a workplace for exchange.

Editorial

Tamachi–Mita pulses with the cadence of the rail line. It is a district defined by Tokyo's velocity, a place where business and urban life intersect at speed. Inside MITAMACHI TERRACE, KOKUYO Co., Ltd. captures this transit culture and gives it a new purpose. The conference facilities and SENQ incubation lounge serve as a contemporary junction, providing a stage for professional connections that feel as vital as the city outside.

Transport references appear through spatial behaviour. Platforms, thresholds, and long counters echo the architecture of a station or a boat dock. Tables serve as grounding elements for focused work. Perimeter benches invite a pause. Clear sightlines maintain order during busy hours. The space borrows the social utility of an interchange to create a setting where people naturally find their rhythm.

Two distinct zones form a deliberate pair. The Workers’ Lounge offers an airy, expansive character. A cool blue wash extends across the high ceiling, echoed in the bespoke artwork and carpeting that evokes water. Light timber, grey upholstery, and rows of adaptable tables provide a neutral backdrop for conferences and gatherings. It is a room designed for the bright, outward energy of the working day.

SENQ Tamachi offers the counterpoint. The ceiling descends. The light shifts to an amber glow. Furniture gathers into protected clusters. A broad communal table anchors the room, while banquettes and lounge chairs foster the intimacy of a private hall. Suspended metal elements hover beneath the ceiling, giving the warm interior a subtle technical edge. It is a space for the quieter, focused work that builds partnerships.

Moving between these zones creates a perceptible shift in pace. The blue lounge supports collective energy, while SENQ encourages conversation and contemplation. Lighting orchestrates this transition. In the lounge, concealed illumination opens the ceiling plane and gives walls a cool horizon. In SENQ, perimeter lighting compresses the room into an intimate field, captured by brass pendants and the central table. A restrained grey passage acts as a pause, a sequence that makes each atmosphere distinct.

The most compelling material gesture surfaces in the commissioned artwork for SENQ. Surplus tile, flooring, and brass baseboards from construction are crushed, melted, and reconfigured. Remnants mix with water-based resin to create panels that preserve chips and grains within graphic fields shaped around the SENQ identity. Waste returns to the site as both ornament and historical record.

This approach connects sustainability to memory. The artwork acknowledges that a new office build leaves a residue. By allowing the process of making to remain visible, the design gives those remains a social position. Installed among meeting areas, the pieces serve as conversation starters, small material histories embedded within a program dedicated to connection.

MITAMACHI TERRACE Conference and SENQ Tamachi succeed as a cohesive pair. One space is airy and adaptable. The other is concentrated and atmospheric. Together, they provide tenants with a wider vocabulary for professional life, from presenting and meeting to solitary thought. The project turns mobility into hospitality, making the workplace junction a destination worth staying in.
