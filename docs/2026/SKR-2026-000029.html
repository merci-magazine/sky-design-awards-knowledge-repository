Project summary

Editorial

A home of this scale is seldom defined by a single lifestyle. It encompasses quiet mornings, family routines, lengthy evenings with friends, and occasions where social gatherings expand without sacrificing privacy. At VILLA 49, Kris Lin International Design embraces this evolving rhythm as the residence's guiding principle.

This four-story villa seamlessly integrates private rooms, social spaces, gardens, and riverside views into a cohesive experience. The architecture, landscaping, interiors, and furnishings were developed in harmony, allowing the home to transition naturally between intimate family life and larger social events.

The dining room exemplifies this concept most clearly. Enclosed on three sides by high-transparency glass, it opens up to lush plantings, water features, and the distant river. Pillar-free corner glazing preserves the expansive view, while oversized movable panels enable the room to adapt to various weather conditions, seasons, and occasions. Whether hosting a family meal or a larger gathering, the dining area can transition fluidly between different degrees of openness. This adaptability enhances the house's lifestyle, fostering hospitality with spaces that can expand informally while preserving private areas through folding doors and movable partitions.

The swimming pool also plays a significant role in connecting daily life with the natural environment. Located in the ground-level garden above the basement, its transparent acrylic structure allows daylight to filter through the water into the social spaces below. This creates reflections that dance across walls and ceilings, maintaining a link to the garden even when the horizon is out of sight.

For anyone spending time in the villa, the effects are immediate. The basement absorbs sunlight, the dining room captures changing river views, and the circulation throughout the house remains intimately connected to water, greenery, and fresh air. The landscape permeates the residence at different levels, extending beyond mere windows. Kris Lin International Design employs transparency with precision. Glass staircases offer a light visual experience, while folding bedroom doors allow private spaces to open onto terraces and broader views. These boundaries are adjustable, granting occupants control over how much of the home they wish to share at any time.

The choice of interior materials supports this sense of continuity. Expansive surfaces of stone, timber, and glass create a calming atmosphere across the spacious rooms, while slender frames, slim drawers, and large curtains maintain a sense of visual lightness. This refinement is experienced through both proportion and touch, keeping the villa’s scale composed. This project is particularly significant in the context of contemporary ideas about luxury, showcasing how space can facilitate the way people gather, retreat, rest, and coexist. VILLA 49 imbues its generosity with purpose by making the house responsive to these varied moments.

IThe home’s most notable feature is the interaction of natural elements throughout the space. Water brings light to the basement, glass walls expand the dining area into the garden, and adjustable rooms allow for both social interaction and privacy, adapting to the rhythm of the day.VILLA 49 exemplifies a way of living where architecture does more than frame a landscape; it actively participates in creating one. Kris Lin International Design designs a residence where light, water, community, and solitude each find their rightful place.
