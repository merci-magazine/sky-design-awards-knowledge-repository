Sienna One Sales Gallery and Clubhouse

SKR-2026-000013 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Sienna One Sales Gallery and Clubhouse, designed by ARTTA Concept Studio, located in Jiaxing, China. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

In Jiaxing, Sienna One pairs a residential sales gallery with a multi-generational clubhouse. ARTTA Concept Studio employs a "Refined Harmony" philosophy, using French Alpine-inspired curves, a signature marble staircase, and seasonal amenity zones to create a durable community setting.

Editorial

In many residential developments, the clubhouse is where the idea of home truly begins to take shape. Before a buyer enters a private apartment, the shared spaces offer a glimpse into daily life: the rhythm of arrival, the quality of light, the texture underfoot, and the atmosphere that exists just beyond the front door. At Sienna One in Jiaxing, ARTTA Concept Studio presents this moment through a thoughtfully designed 3,550-square-metre sales gallery and clubhouse that embodies the idea of refined luxury.

The concept of "Refined Harmony" draws inspiration from the landscape and seasonal essence of the French Alps, evoking images of open air and a life balanced between activity and tranquillity. ARTTA Concept Studio translates these impressions into curved architecture, warm whites, bronze accents, natural marble, and timber, creating a calm environment amid the pace of contemporary residential life.

This expression of luxury prioritizes ease and comfort. Spaces feel generous yet approachable, with materials chosen for how they engage with light, touch, and movement. This atmosphere invites prospective residents to experience the development as much through sensory engagement as through presentation.

The entrance features a darker, more intimate setting, with curved ceiling fins and bronze details providing a transitional moment before opening into a luminous reception space. Here, pale marble and warm white surfaces create a serene backdrop for art and furnishings. This shift in brightness offers a gentle sense of ceremony, marking the transition from the city's hustle to the clubhouse's tranquil social environment.

Near the reception, small hospitality touches create a personal touch. A high table, orange stools, and clustered globe lights invite brief pauses, whether for a meeting or a casual drink. These elements suggest the potential for future interactions among members, establishing a polished yet familiar ambiance.

At the heart of the project, the Fluid Arc Staircase embodies the understated luxury envisioned for Sienna One. Carved from solid marble, it sweeps beneath an oval skylight, infusing the space with natural light and generous volume. Its refined design enhances the journey between floors, transforming everyday movement into a composed experience. The staircase serves as a central feature, visible from various points within the clubhouse, enhancing awareness of activities in surrounding areas and fostering a sense of community.

The lounges reflect a contemporary understanding of shared living, allowing members the option to connect with the surrounding life while enjoying moments of solitude. ARTTA Concept Studio has tailored each facility to embody a seasonal character. The gym, inspired by winter, features cool tones and timber to evoke a mountainous feel, while the ball court channels summer's vibrancy through energetic colours and lighting. Spring is embodied in the children's playground with soft forms and colours, and autumn finds its place in the changing rooms through rich timber and warm hues.

Members experience the clubhouse as a series of environments, each attuned to various times of the day, unified by material consistency. Natural marble is arranged in flowing patterns, creating a sense of continuity across the floors. Hand-finished wall surfaces interact with varying daylight, and brushed metals add warmth to the pale interiors, enriching the overall sensory experience.

Sienna One reflects the growing importance of the residential clubhouse in Chinese cities, where shared amenities redefine concepts of home, community, and individual wellbeing. These spaces facilitate a gentle merging of private life and social engagement, nurturing connections among neighbours through shared activities and everyday interactions. With inspiration from the French Alpine landscape, ARTTA Concept Studio envisions a harmonious living environment that seamlessly integrates art, leisure, craftsmanship, and comfort, elevating luxury to encompass everyday life through thoughtful attention to light, space, and materials.
