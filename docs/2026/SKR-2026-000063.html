SKR-2026-000063

Club Anonymous

SKR-2026-000063 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Club Anonymous, designed by Adapa Architects Limited, located in Hong Kong. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

Behind an unmarked Hong Kong doorway, Adapa Architects composes a private club as a sequence of raw rooms, luminous salons and sheltered corners calibrated for different degrees of company.

Editorial

Club Anonymous represents a departure from conventional members' clubs, particularly within the context of Hong Kong, where exclusivity is often signified through visible signage and elaborate ceremonies. Adapa Architects Limited has strategically eliminated nearly all external indicators of the club. The entrance features an unmarked black steel door, leading to a narrow vaulted passage and a singular circular opening. This design choice establishes an initial distance, allowing the bustling city outside to recede into the background.

This decision is significant as the 6,000-square-foot interior is constructed around a distinctive interpretation of privacy. Membership is not indicated by grand reception areas or formal processions; rather, the club is organized around varying degrees of participation. This arrangement enables individuals to transition smoothly between activities such as conversation, retreat, dining, and events, without the constraints of rigid spatial definitions for each setting.

The main living room immediately exemplifies this principle of freedom. A broad modular sofa occupies the central area, creating an adaptable social landscape, complemented by a bar that shares the same visual field. Exposed concrete beams, unfinished textures, and precisely crafted arches prevent the room from appearing overly polished, while a delicate glass chandelier introduces an element of fragility overhead. This contrast imbues the space with character without rendering it contrived.

The informality inherent in this design is central to the attraction of the club. Members may arrive for a drink, linger at the bar, engage in larger conversations, or settle into one of the sofa groupings. The architectural design does not prescribe specific uses for the room; instead, it allows interpersonal relationships to dictate the utilization of the space.

The Sun Lounge introduces a marked change in atmosphere. With travertine flooring, reclaimed timber, deeper upholstery, and a central fireplace, it cultivates a warmer, more domestic ambiance. The adjoining terrace enhances this sense of relaxation, making the space suitable for both leisurely afternoons and intimate conversations without completely detaching members from the club’s social environment.

In contrast, the private dining room sharpens the overall mood. Its black surfaces, antique mirrored ceiling, and concentrated lighting create a deliberately ceremonial atmosphere around the table. This impact is heightened by the contrast with the adjacent spaces, as the club intentionally avoids a uniform aesthetic. Instead, it employs varying atmospheres to facilitate different modes of engagement.

An internal courtyard adds further complexity to this social structure. An artificial skylight ensures even illumination for the plant life and patterned stone, while function rooms and a cooking studio open toward it. What may have been merely a circulation area takes on its own identity, serving as a venue where diverse activities can intersect. Events can extend into this space, conversations can persist, and individuals moving between rooms remain connected to the broader club environment.

Consistent features such as repeated arches, circular openings, and framed views reinforce a sense of gradual revelation. Rooms are seldom fully visible at first glance; instead, their edges become perceptible first, followed by glimpses of light, furnishings, or other groups beyond. Privacy, therefore, is managed through spatial distance, angles, and partial visibility, rather than solely by means of closed doors.

Club Anonymous serves as a compelling contemporary membership culture. Its luxury is not embedded in ostentation or material wealth but in the empowerment of individuals to control their visibility, sociability, or withdrawal in any given moment. Adapa Architects effectively transforms the concept of anonymity into a spatial principle, featuring a discreet entrance, a progression of changing moods, and a club environment where privacy is delineated not merely by separation, but by individual choice.
