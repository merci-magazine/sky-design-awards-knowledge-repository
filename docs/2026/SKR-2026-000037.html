SKR-2026-000037

Lakeside Villa

SKR-2026-000037 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Lakeside Villa, designed by WJ STUDIO / Hu Zhile, located in Shanghai, China. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

WJ STUDIO’s Lakeside Villa redefines luxury residential architecture in Shanghai. By eliminating the boundaries between private gallery and home, this fluid, light-filled masterpiece offers a blueprint for modern collectors seeking to house art within the daily rhythm of life.

Editorial

A personal art collection often reflects more than just artistic taste; it encapsulates moments from one's life, whether recalling a journey, a relationship, or the time when an object first gained significance. At Lakeside Villa in Shanghai, WJ STUDIO has designed a home where these memories are integrated into the fabric of daily life.

Known as "My Gallery Home," this 550-square-meter residence was created for an experienced collector whose works had been in storage for years. The design reintroduces these pieces, allowing art, daily living, and the surrounding landscape to form an evolving composition.

The experience begins at the entrance, where a glass pavilion serves as a private gallery adjacent to the garden. Selected pieces are illuminated by natural light, enhanced by reflections of greenery and changes in the seasons. This pavilion offers each visitor a moment of reconnection, inviting them to engage with the collection before returning to the home's more intimate areas.

The pavilion's transparency alters the character of the display. Art is observed alongside plants, water features, and shifting weather, allowing the setting to evolve throughout the day. The gallery seamlessly integrates into the landscape and becomes part of the resident's daily experience.

Moving deeper into the home, WJ STUDIO eliminates conventional door frames, aligning openings throughout the space. Living areas, corridors, and display spaces flow into one another, enabling art to coexist naturally with furniture and familiar pathways used for conversation and relaxation.

This spatial continuity mirrors how personal collections are formed over time through individual encounters. Each piece tells its own story while remaining connected to the life surrounding it. Lakeside Villa grants the collection a similar freedom, allowing individual works to capture attention without isolating them from the broader context of the home.

Daylight plays a crucial role in this relationship. Skylights bring natural light into the deeper areas of the house, creating a connection between the upper and lower floors through shifting patterns of brightness. As the sun moves, colours, textures, and shadows transform, offering new perspectives on familiar artworks.

The interior colour palette maintains a serene atmosphere for the collection while preserving the warmth of a private residence. Wood, textiles, and familiar domestic objects introduce softness to the rooms, ensuring that the art remains part of an inhabited environment characterized by comfort, conversation, and daily use.

Views of the garden and lake add another dimension of change. The landscape follows its own seasonal rhythm, providing a living counterpart to the objects collected over many years. Art, architecture, and nature engage in a continuous dialogue, each offering a different measure of time.

WJ STUDIO emphasizes the relationship between viewing and living. The entrance pavilion provides a focused experience with select pieces, while the interior allows the collection to accompany the resident throughout the day. A specific piece may be observed formally at one moment and noticed casually from a familiar chair at another. This proximity gives the house an emotional character. The collection is seen as part of the owner's life story, embodying memories and experiences in the present. The architecture creates a setting where these connections can continue to grow as new works and new chapters in life are added.

Lakeside Villa illustrates collecting as a dynamic and personal way to shape a home. Through light, openness, and the gradual movement between gallery, living space, and garden, WJ STUDIO has crafted a residence where art is continually rediscovered within the changing patterns of everyday life.
