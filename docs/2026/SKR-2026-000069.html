Editorial

In Tokyo's Kyobashi district, the traditional showroom experience, often filled with price lists and cabinetry dimensions, is changing significantly. TOCLAS SALON TOKYO, designed by Mitsui Designtec, focuses on brand storytelling as a key pathway to product discovery. This flagship space encourages visitors to connect with the TOCLAS worldview before they even consider the practicalities of the products.

Mitsui Designtec has structured this journey through a purposeful two-zone sequence. Upon entering, the Entrance Lounge sets the stage for important brand values like Surprise, Inspiration, Peace of Mind, and Timelessness. This emotional grounding prepares visitors for the Exhibition Area, where technical innovations take center stage. This flow guides visitors from an initial impression of the brand to a deeper understanding of its product capabilities.

The design of the space plays a vital role in this experience. Situated within the Kyobashi Kinoterrace, the salon reflects the building's exposed timber-and-steel framework. These wooden beams serve as the primary design language, showcasing the brand’s commitment to sustainability. By highlighting the structural framework, Mitsui Designtec ensures the salon has a clear identity based on material honesty and avoids a cluttered appearance.

This design approach elevates the visit from a simple transaction to an engaging brand experience. Brand identity provides essential context, while the technical displays illustrate how this philosophy fits into everyday domestic life. In this environment, architecture lends credibility, the atmosphere draws attention, and technology enriches the design narrative, demonstrating how a manufacturer can connect seamlessly with modern living.
