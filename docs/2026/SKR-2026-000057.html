Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai Renewal

SKR-2026-000057 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai Renewal, designed by SPACE CO., LTD. / Yuta Hoshi, located in Sendai, Miyagi, Japan. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

SPACE CO., LTD. has completed a comprehensive interior renovation of the Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai, which features biophilic design, dynamic lighting systems, and water-inspired architecture. This hospitality project, set to be finished in 2025, updates urban hotel standards in Sendai, Japan.

Editorial

Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai is deeply influenced by Sendai's identity as the City of Trees. After sixteen years in operation, the hotel has undergone a renovation by SPACE CO., LTD. that integrates this natural character into the guest experience through thoughtful landscaping, filtered light, and water features. This aligns with Mitsui Garden Hotels' broader “Stay in the Garden” philosophy.

The renovation concept, “Light Place,” draws inspiration from the outdoor ambiance of Sendai. On streets such as Jozenji-dori, sunlight filters through the canopies of zelkova trees, creating intricate patterns on the pavement. SPACE translates this experience into the hotel through dynamic lighting, reflective elements, and carefully positioned greenery.

The lobby is where this concept is most prominently showcased. Layers of lush planting create depth around the arrival area, while expansive windows keep guests connected to the city outside. Seating is thoughtfully distributed among the greenery, allowing the public space to have a gentle rhythm that shifts throughout the day as natural and artificial light change.

Behind the reception desk, a dark perforated screen reflects the dappled light found beneath the trees in Sendai. This theme continues through the hotel’s circulation areas, where discreet lighting highlights blue-toned artwork, linking the lobby to the guest floors visually and thematically.

In the guestrooms, a more subdued approach is taken. Warm timber, light surfaces, and muted blue-grey tones create a calm and inviting atmosphere. Subtle leaf-like designs and indirect lighting reinforce the garden theme on an intimate scale. The ambiance and colour palette ensure that the rooms remain restful and easy to inhabit.

Water features play a central role in the public bathing areas. Warm and cool lighting lend distinct moods to each space, while illuminated wall panels mimic the effects of light on water, creating a sense of movement and reflection. The adjacent grooming and waiting areas continue this design language, incorporating plants, comfortable seating, and warm light to enhance the overall experience.

This interplay of trees, light, and water provides a distinct regional identity across multiple areas of the hotel. The design references are varied enough to cater to different functions arrival, circulation, sleeping, and bathing ensuring continuity while avoiding a homogeneous look.

The renewal of Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai reflects the evolving expectations of urban guests. In addition to practical comfort, the revitalized public spaces now offer a stronger sense of place and a more restorative atmosphere amid a busy schedule of appointments and sightseeing.

The Mitsui Garden Hotel Sendai Renewal weaves the essence of the City of Trees into the fabric of its hospitality experience. Through a thoughtful blend of light, planting, water, and materials, SPACE CO., LTD. has fostered a renewed connection to Sendai while preserving the comfort and familiarity that guests expect from a well-established property.
