CAPELLAMORPH — Flexible Metal Sheet

SKR-2026-000028 is the Sky Knowledge Record for CAPELLAMORPH — Flexible Metal Sheet, designed by SOL style, located in Japan. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

Transform interiors with CAPELLAMORPH: the next generation of high-performance metal sheeting. This innovative material combines gradient powder coating with structural flexibility, enabling designers to create responsive walls and ceilings that dynamically interact with light and shadow.

Editorial

Materials typically enter a project after the initial spatial concept has been established. CAPELLAMORPH encourages designers to begin with the surface itself, considering color, light, and geometry as one cohesive architectural decision. Developed by SOL style in collaboration with Sirius Co., Ltd., this aluminum-composite sheet combines low weight, ease of workability, and a proprietary powder-coating process. Its significance lies in how these qualities work together. A single panel can carry a gradient, hold a fold, catch changing light, and extend across walls or ceilings as part of a broader spatial composition.

The material's colour is never entirely fixed; gradients shift depending on the viewing angle, surrounding light, and the shape of the sheet itself. A fold can intensify one hue while deepening another into shadow, creating a new visual experience as people move through the space. The visual effect evolves with ordinary shifts in position and the passage of time. This gives CAPELLAMORPH an architectural character that goes beyond mere surface decoration. The colour comes from the coating, while depth is derived from the formed metal and its interaction with light. Features such as wrinkling, embossing, curves, and folds introduce additional variation, allowing a static material to create a dynamic atmosphere.

For designers, the appeal of CAPELLAMORPH is both practical and aesthetic. The sheet can be shaped into sculptural forms while still allowing for manageable fabrication and installation. Custom lengths and seamless joining allow several panels to read as a single continuous surface, transforming the material from a singular feature into an architectural element. Its lightweight nature expands the range of potential applications, making ceilings, elevated walls, temporary installations, and larger suspended forms easier to handle and support. Additionally, its modularity allows sections to be replaced or updated, making the system adaptable to evolving interiors and event environments.

The material is particularly effective in spaces where atmosphere is as important as function. For instance, a retail interior might use a gradual shift in colour to guide movement, while an exhibition setting could leverage the same surface to change between daylight and evening illumination. In a public space, a series of repeated folded panels could establish a rhythm without relying on screens or digital effects.

CAPELLAMORPH also incorporates environmental considerations into its material system. The powder coating process reduces VOC emissions, aluminum offers a well-established recycling route, and the lower weight minimizes transport demands and the need for secondary support structures. These qualities link visual expression with efficiencies in fabrication, installation, and future replacement.

The broader relevance of this material lies in its ability to consolidate functions. One surface can provide colour, texture, reflection, and three-dimensional form while remaining integral to a practical construction process. It offers architects and interior designers a material that can shape a room's atmosphere through shifting light and movement.

CAPELLAMORPH transforms metal from a background finish into an active spatial medium. Its identity is defined by the interaction of colour, form, and time, giving designers a new way to create interiors that continue to evolve after installation.
