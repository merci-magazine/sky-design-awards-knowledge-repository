BRANZ Mega Kuningan & Swissôtel Living Jakarta Mega Kuningan

SKR-2026-000055 is the Sky Knowledge Record for BRANZ Mega Kuningan & Swissôtel Living Jakarta Mega Kuningan, designed by FIELD FOUR DESIGN OFFICE, located in Mega Kuningan, Jakarta, Indonesia. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

FIELD FOUR DESIGN OFFICE transforms Jakarta’s Mega Kuningan skyline with BRANZ Mega Kuningan and Swissôtel Living. This mixed-use project redefines urban luxury through a sophisticated sequence of planted terraces, vertical louvres, and tactile plaster interiors, establishing a seamless, serene transition between the bustling commercial district and private residential sanctuary.

Editorial

In Jakarta, the journey home often begins long before reaching the front door. The city's traffic, commercial density, and vast scale are ever-present as residents transition from the street into the tower. At BRANZ Mega Kuningan and Swissôtel Living, FIELD FOUR DESIGN OFFICE has designed this transition to be part of daily life, creating a gradual shift from the bustling business district to a calmer residential environment. This mixed-use development brings together two distinct forms of urban living. One tower houses the BRANZ condominium residences by Tokyu Land Corporation, while the other is occupied by Swissôtel Living, managed by Accor. Although these two addresses cater to different residents, they share a landscape-oriented entry experience characterized by greenery, screened views, and a thoughtful sequence of spaces.

The project’s “Urban Oasis” is revealed through movement. Greenery initially appears in glimpses and becomes more prominent as residents and guests move deeper inside. Vertical louvres soften direct views, passages fluctuate between narrow and wide, and changes in scale slow the pace of arrival. The city remains close, yet its intensity diminishes.

FIELD FOUR draws on a material familiarity shared by the domestic cultures of Japan and Indonesia. Using wood, earth-toned plaster, stone, and planting, the interiors exude warmth without relying on decorative elements. The connection between these two cultures is conveyed through texture, shadow, and proportion, allowing the spaces to feel rooted in both traditions while firmly situated in contemporary Jakarta. The vertical louvres play a crucial role in creating this atmosphere. They provide privacy without complete separation, allowing light and movement to remain visible throughout the interior. As residents pass by daily, these surfaces appear solid, transparent, or layered depending on one’s viewpoint, giving the shared spaces a dynamic character.

The journey culminates at the eleventh-floor Sky Lobby, where the two towers converge around a planted pool. This elevated area houses the condominium and serviced-apartment lobbies, restaurant, and gym, creating a shared central space between the buildings. Water, vegetation, and open sky contribute to a sense of release from the towers 'enclosed environment. For residents, the garden becomes part of the daily commute home. For guests, it serves as an immediate introduction to the essence of the address. People can wait, meet, dine, or pass through while remaining visually linked to the landscape. The amenities are integrated into the building's life, rather than being hidden away down corridors.

Mirrored columns along the perimeter reflect snippets of water, greenery, and shifting daylight. These surfaces minimize the structure's visual weight and allow the atmosphere to evolve throughout the day. In the evening, warmer lighting enhances the intimacy of the seating areas while the plantings remain visible against the illuminated interiors. The condominium and serviced residences maintain their unique identities. Shared materials and landscaping foster a sense of belonging to the same development, while variations in enclosure, furniture, and atmosphere cater to both permanent residents and short-stay guests. The two experiences remain connected without merging into one another.

BRANZ Mega Kuningan and Swissôtel Living present an urban retreat that facilitates a daily transition. FIELD FOUR DESIGN OFFICE has crafted a space where residents and guests can gradually leave the rapid pace of Jakarta behind, moving through shade, texture, and landscape before finally arriving at the comfort of home. The project’s sense of luxury arises from this change of pace: a few thoughtfully composed moments between city life and domestic tranquillity.
