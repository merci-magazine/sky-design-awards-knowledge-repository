Panasonic Group Pavilion “The Land of NOMO”

SKR-2026-000020 is the Sky Knowledge Record for Panasonic Group Pavilion “The Land of NOMO”, designed by NOMURA Co., Ltd., located in Expo 2025 Osaka, Yumeshima, Japan. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

The Land of NOMO at Expo 2025 Osaka: A sensory-driven pavilion by NOMURA for Panasonic. This experiential space eliminates fixed paths, utilizing light, sound, and biodegradable architecture to engage 470,000 visitors, including 115,000 schoolchildren, in unscripted discovery.

Editorial

At Expo 2025 Osaka, Panasonic’s pavilion, The Land of NOMO, is a captivating celebration of the company’s vision, inviting visitors to step into a vibrant world filled with movement, sensation, and the thrill of personal exploration. Created by the innovative team at NOMURA Co., Ltd., this stunning pavilion, under the inspiring theme “Unlock Your Nature,” draws upon the beauty of forests, ancient trees, and crystals to craft an extraordinary landscape that comes alive through touch, light, mist, and sound.

This imaginative concept takes root from the ideals of Panasonic's founder, Konosuke Matsushita, who wholeheartedly believed in the unique talents that reside within each person. NOMURA has beautifully brought this philosophy to life by designing a space that encourages open-ended exploration, allowing visitors to set their own paths and make choices that shape their journey.

Central to this pavilion's charm is a spirit of openness. The custom floor tiles mimic the natural, uneven surfaces of a forest floor, while tree-like structures and crystal-inspired forms create an inviting atmosphere that feels both familiar and ripe for individual interpretation. With the help of advanced computational design and digital fabrication, the pavilion breathes with branching geometries that evoke organic growth, steering clear of a literal representation of nature.

As guests wander through the pavilion, they will be surrounded by a harmonious interplay of light, sound, and mist, creating an atmosphere responsive to their movements and presence. The integration of technology enhances the experience by igniting curiosity and fostering emotional connections, elegantly woven into the environment without overshadowing its natural essence.

Throughout their exploration, visitors will find their actions observed, culminating in a personalized narrative that reflects their unique journey. Integrating mirrors within the tree-like forms offers a moment of self-recognition, allowing guests to connect their sensory experiences with personal insights.

The design brilliantly caters to diverse styles of participation. With a focus on engaging the senses through texture, movement, sound, and light, everyone can immerse themselves in the experience without needing extensive verbal or written instructions. A calming room is thoughtfully positioned along the pathway for those seeking a quieter space, providing a sanctuary to recharge and rejoin the experience at their own pace.

Beyond the enchanting design, Panasonic emphasizes sustainability through mindful material choices. Elements crafted from leaf-like mesh and tree structures are made using Kinari, a biodegradable cellulose-fibre resin tinted with compostable pigments. The design minimizes waste and ensures modular floor tiles can be repurposed after the Expo, demonstrating our commitment to our planet.

Years of dedicated workshops and a focus on observed behaviour have shaped this inspiring environment. The design enables discovery without dictating outcomes, celebrating sensation and movement as meaningful pathways to understanding.

Welcoming an audience of 470,000, including 115,000 enthusiastic schoolchildren, The Land of NOMO showcased Panasonic’s human-centred philosophy to a wide array of visitors. This pavilion harmoniously intertwines technology, inclusivity, and environmental responsibility into a cohesive experience, illustrating how architecture can inspire attention, choice, and personal interpretation.

NOMURA has created a pavilion that is truly defined by its openness. The Land of NOMO offers a carefully crafted environment that evolves with each visitor, allowing them to create unique memories, experiences, and meanings within its enchanting landscape.
