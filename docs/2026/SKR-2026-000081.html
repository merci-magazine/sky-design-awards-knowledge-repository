A Chromatic Rhapsody: Reimagining the Modern Dwelling

SKR-2026-000081 is the Sky Knowledge Record for A Chromatic Rhapsody: Reimagining the Modern Dwelling, designed by Deco Farmer Studio, located in Hong Kong. The project is shortlisted / finalist for Sky Design Awards 2026.

Project summary

Deco Farmer Studio transforms a Hong Kong apartment into a high-impact residential design. By leveraging primary colour blocks, strategic joinery, and custom geometric surfaces, the layout redefines traditional living spaces into a gallery-focused interior.

Editorial

Colour establishes the rhythm of "A Chromatic Rhapsody" from the moment you enter. Designed by Hong Kong-based Deco Farmer Studio, the residence uses yellow, green, red, and blue as instruments of spatial identity. Each colour corresponds to a surface, object, or threshold, allowing this compact domestic interior to convey the energy of a much larger space.

The conventional living room is reimagined as an expansive foyer. A bicycle is showcased against a geometric tiled wall, transforming an everyday object into a work of art and introducing the owners through the items they use. This playful gesture also alters how the home is perceived: the entryway, display, and social spaces overlap from the beginning.

Throughout the shared areas, loose furniture and built-in elements follow the same colour scheme. Doors, cabinets, and shelves are each assigned distinct blocks of colour, while patterned surfaces highlight the junctions between them. The colour palette is vibrant, yet its application remains deliberate. Large planes create visual order, while smaller accents introduce movement and prevent the interior from settling into a singular decorative style.

This careful control is especially evident in the guest room, which is divided into a man cave and a walk-in wardrobe. A red feature screen separates the two areas without completely closing them off. Textured wall coverings and contrasting colours provide each side with a unique character, turning a practical division into one of the project’s most expressive sequences.

The design also recognizes the importance of release. As the layout moves toward the owners’ bedroom, the colour palette softens, and the pace slows. Calmer tones signal privacy and rest, completing the transition from the lively public areas to a more tranquil personal retreat. Thus, the chromatic narrative has a clear beginning, crescendo, and conclusion.

Deco Farmer Studio utilizes colour to reflect personality while maintaining everyday functionality. Storage is thoughtfully integrated, circulation paths are clear, and personal objects are intentionally placed within the overall composition. While the residence feels curated, it doesn’t resemble a showroom. Its most compelling quality is the sense that the colours have emerged from the owners’ habits, sense of humour, and appreciation for visual vibrancy.

"A Chromatic Rhapsody" presents domestic exuberance as a form of discipline. The project’s confidence stems from a clear understanding of where colour should dominate, where texture should add depth, and where quieter surfaces are needed. This balance transforms a bold palette into a livable environment and gives the home an immediate, personal identity that resonates throughout the entire space.
