## Editorial Rewrite

Toscana in Discovery Bay serves as a sanctuary designed by ARTTA Concept Studio, transforming the 1,500-square-meter site into a welcoming, home-like atmosphere for potential residents. The design orchestrates a journey for families, outlining their possible daily routines.
Arches form the core of the layout. The reception thresholds and meeting room doors echo this archway design in various sizes, establishing a cohesive rhythm that soothes pedestrian movement. Material choices play a crucial role in the overall aesthetic. Grey leather pairs with camel upholstery, while limestone and Italian marble delineate edges, providing depth and tactile variety that enhance visual interest. The Tuscan inspiration permeates the environment, capturing the essence of a home.
Upon arrival, visitors are presented with a framed experience. Bronze and stone anchor the work desk, while curved openings direct attention toward the central model area. Soft lighting graces the floor, ensuring that models are displayed at precise heights to illustrate site relationships effectively. Circulation flows smoothly, allowing groups to gather comfortably.
A café and outdoor patio extend the visitor experience, incorporating hospitality within the same narrative as the models. Guests are encouraged to linger, and exterior connections create a shift in ambiance. Digital renderings transition into tangible outdoor access, while the gym concept introduces an active element. Biophilic exercise equipment enables visitors to engage with proposed wellness features, transforming concepts into physical interactions.
A dedicated corridor leads to the VIP quarters, where patterned carpets and warm timber create a more intimate atmosphere, indicating a shift from public information to private discussions. The VIP rooms utilize leather wall coverings and carefully selected furniture to create a contained and comfortable setting. These rooms serve a sales purpose while allowing prospective residents to envision their own privacy.
The gallery succeeds in balancing aspiration with practical information. Models and consultation areas are kept separate, while the café and exercise facilities introduce familiar rituals that the development aims to promote. Toscana offers more than just a property; it provides a sequence of experiences that convey a desirable lifestyle. Each stage reveals tangible evidence of the life prospective buyers could lead. Arches impose a sense of order, natural materials foster tranquillity, and outdoor amenities link the sales experience to daily living, allowing prospective buyers to preview the community they might become a part of.

## Editor’s Review

The project achieves harmony by integrating various sales functions within a cohesive architectural style. Each area serves a specific purpose while contributing to an overall unified atmosphere. Recurring arches delineate the space, guiding visitors and marking transitions between public and private areas.
This approach fosters a strong identity, emphasizing structural proportions rather than superficial decoration. The outdoor terrace and exercise area enhance the project by allowing visitors to engage directly with the proposed lifestyle. These features transform residential concepts into tangible opportunities for relaxation and activity.

## Drawing and Spatial Analysis

The submitted zoning plan delineates a cohesive flow starting from the reception area and leading to key spaces, including the central model display, café, outdoor patio, virtual demonstration room, and VIP consultation areas.
Central to the plan, public information is prominently featured, with model tables arranged to ensure clear circulation paths from multiple angles. This design allows both visitors and staff to interact comfortably while facilitating unobstructed movement throughout the space.
The hospitality area is oriented towards the exterior, seamlessly integrating café seating with the outdoor patio, creating a vibrant and accessible environment. The "Gym Everywhere" equipment extends the hospitality area, enhancing the active landscape amenity.
Access to the VIP rooms is provided through a dedicated corridor adorned with arches, which enhances privacy while creating a visual link to the gallery's architectural style.
The virtual demonstration room is thoughtfully enclosed to control light and sound, allowing for effective presentations. Its close proximity to the model display and consultation areas encourages a natural flow from physical exhibits to digital demonstrations and in-depth discussions.
While the submission lacks detailed diagrams, renderings, or model specifications, it effectively establishes key spatial relationships and outlines the intended functionality of the various zones within the plan.

## Original Designer Submission — Verbatim

Toscana in Discovery Bay Sales Gallery\_ ARTTA Concept Studio
A Sophisticated Lifestyle, Inspired by Nature
The overarching design concept is inspired by Toscana, Italy, with the goal of creating a "peaceful getaway" for visitors. The design aims to recreate the feeling of a sophisticated Tuscan home by using elements of nature, rich earth tones, and textured surfaces. The target clientele for this development are young families and middle-class individuals seeking a quality and sophisticated lifestyle.
A key architectural element drawn from Tuscan architecture is the arched window frame, which is used to symbolize a combination of functionality and aesthetics. The color and material palettes are consistent with this theme, featuring naturally found, soothing earth tones like grey leather, limestone, camel-colored leather, Italian marble, and silver travertine. The inclusion of bronze material is meant to symbolize fine craftsmanship and the luxury living offered at the development.
The proposed zoning plan details a variety of functional spaces, including a reception area, a central model zone, a cafe with an outdoor patio, VIP meeting rooms, and a virtual demonstration dark room. The design also incorporates a unique "Gym Everywhere" concept, with an area on the outdoor patio dedicated to trying out biophilic gym equipment, enhancing the connection to nature and a luxury lifestyle.
Detailed renderings and mood boards for each area visualize the final design, from the material callouts at the reception to the furniture options in the VIP rooms. The proposal extends its detailed design ethos into more private spaces. A dedicated corridor, featuring arched doorways and pattern carpet, leads clients to several VIP and meeting rooms. These rooms show intimate and luxurious settings, finished with materials like timber veneer and wallcoverings to provide a comfortable environment for sales discussions.
This meticulous planning is further underscored by the detailed model checklist, which specifies the scale, table height, and purpose of each architectural model, ensuring a clear and informative presentation for prospective buyers. By consistently applying key architectural elements like arched frames and a carefully curated palette of natural materials such as limestone, Italian marble, and rich leathers, the design creates a soothing and calming atmosphere for its target clientele of young families and the middle class.
Every zone, from the public reception to the private VIP rooms, is crafted to embody a "sophisticated lifestyle". Ultimately, this proposal delivers more than just a sales office; it offers a fully immersive experience designed to feel like a "peaceful getaway," giving prospective buyers a tangible preview of the quality of life awaiting them at the development.
