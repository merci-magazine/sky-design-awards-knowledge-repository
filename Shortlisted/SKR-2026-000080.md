## Excerpt

NOMURA Co., Ltd. is transforming Osaka's urban gateway by integrating the retail interiors of GRAND GREEN OSAKA with Umekita Park through responsive kinetic art, strategic lighting, and immersive biophilic design.

## Editorial

At GRAND GREEN OSAKA, the delineation between the shopping district, station concourse, and urban park has been purposefully softened. In the South Commercial Zone, NOMURA Co., Ltd. has designed the interior as an integral component of Osaka’s larger pedestrian landscape, where retail, food, art, and public circulation collaboratively exist within the same civic environment.
 
This development is situated within one of the city’s most prominent mixed-use projects, directly connected to Osaka Station and Umekita Park. This strategic positioning influences the character of the commercial floors, featuring spacious pathways, visually accessible entrances, and long vistas that guide the eye toward greenery, terraces, and adjacent public spaces. The overall experience is akin to traversing a neighbourhood rather than entering a conventional shopping center.
 
The integration of nature is articulated through both landscaping and artistic interpretation. In one atrium, hundreds of suspended reflective elements evoke the appearance of leaves suspended in mid-air. Their polished surfaces fragment the surrounding architecture, people, and shifting light, creating a dynamic canopy that continuously evolves as visitors traverse the space beneath it. Additionally, sweeping bands of illuminated vertical structures delineate graceful curves across the ceiling, introducing a rhythmic elegance to major circulation areas and offering a distinctive identity to the concourse from afar.
 
This relationship with the landscape is expressed through a vertical arrangement of four distinct themes: Mineral on B1, Grove on 1F, Breeze on 2F, and Daylight on 3F. Each level distinctly conveys its own atmosphere while preserving a coherent connection to the broader theme of nature inherent in the development. The lower floors are characterized by stone-like textures and deeper tones, while the middle levels incorporate plantings and more inviting materials. The upper circulation areas are defined by brighter surfaces and more open ceiling configurations.
 
The B1 level represents the vibrant energy of an urban market, featuring Time Out Market Osaka, along with various cafés and Japanese restaurants. This area showcases a darker, more dense interior, where exposed ceilings, warm pendant lighting, and graphic signage create a distinctly social environment. This contrasts effectively with the brighter retail floors above, offering a diverse range of experiences that include quick dining options, evening gatherings, as well as shopping for fashion, jewelry, and opportunities for leisurely browsing.
 
Art is seamlessly integrated at multiple scales. Reflective ceiling installations invigorate major public pathways, while sculptural elements are strategically positioned near restaurants and along quieter passageways. This thoughtful placement allows cultural expressions to become part of the daily movement within the complex, encountered while navigating between shops, awaiting appointments, or proceeding toward the park.
 
The external architecture further reinforces this integrative relationship. Landscaped terraces, planted borders, and pedestrian courts occupy the spaces between the commercial podium and surrounding towers, effectively extending the retail environment into the public realm of GRAND GREEN OSAKA. From within, glazed openings and garden views maintain this connection, while from the park, the commercial levels present an animated edge to the larger urban district.
 
The most noteworthy aspect of the South Commercial Zone is the harmonious coexistence of these varied elements. Luxury retail, restaurants, public art, landscaped courtyards, and major commuting pathways each maintain distinct identities without devolving into isolated destinations. NOMURA Co., Ltd. has crafted a commercial interior that actively engages with the life of the district, thus extending the experience of GRAND GREEN OSAKA from the park and streets into the spaces where individuals shop, dine, convene, and spend their leisure time.
