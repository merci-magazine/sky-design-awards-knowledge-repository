## Excerpt

Ryubo Food Hall by SPACE CO., LTD. redefines Naha commercial interiors. A strategic lighting design shifts this department store floor from a high-efficiency lunch hub to a polished evening dining destination.

## Editorial

A department store often reflects the rhythm of the city surrounding it, bringing together shopping, dining, conversation, and everyday encounters under one roof. On the second floor of Ryubo Department Store, the RYUBO FOOD HALL enhances this role with a contemporary dining space that connects to the working and social life of Naha.
SPACE CO., LTD. transformed the former retail floor into a food hall that evolves throughout the day. Lunchtime buzzes with energy from nearby offices and department store activities, while afternoons offer a more relaxed atmosphere. In the evening, the space takes on a warmer character, focused on dining and drinks. These overlapping rhythms integrate the hall into Naha's daily routine. The project is guided by the concept of "Contrast," which is demonstrated through the changing interplay of light, time, and atmosphere. Natural daylight and even illumination provide clarity to the interior during the day, while indirect lighting and pendant lamps introduce a greater warmth in the evening, allowing the architecture to adapt without losing its identity.
This transformation gives the food hall a unique temporal quality, as the interior responds to the city's rhythm, carrying the brightness of the workday into a more intimate evening setting. Light becomes an integral part of the hospitality experience, influencing how the space is perceived as the hours change. Design Team involvement encompassed planning, design, construction, and leasing support. This comprehensive approach enabled the development of a cohesive environment for tenant composition, circulation, seating, service requirements, and visual identity. The food hall was designed as a complete destination within the department store, integrating commercial planning with spatial experience.
Each tenant maintains a distinct presence, while a unified material and graphic language provides continuity throughout the floor. This balance allows various culinary offerings to remain visible within a consistent setting, enhancing the collective identity of RYUBO FOOD HALL. The atmosphere combines the composure and consistency of traditional department-store hospitality with a more contemporary social setting. Clear sightlines, well-measured lighting, and an organized spatial layout contribute to a sense of ease throughout the space.
This setting reflects the essence of Okinawa, particularly as it relates to present-day Naha. The food hall caters to those who work, shop, meet, and spend time in the city, offering visitors a glimpse into its everyday urban culture. Local identity is shaped through the use of the space, quality of service, and the varying pace of daily life.
As a result, Ryubo assumes a broader role within the city. Dining and conversation extend the department store's function beyond retail, creating a space where different segments of Naha's community can come together throughout the day. The Ryubo Food Hall showcases department-store renewal as a unified hospitality experience. Through thoughtful design in lighting, service, tenant planning, and brand identity, SPACE CO., LTD. has developed a dining area that seamlessly transitions with Naha's rhythm, from the bustling energy of lunchtime to the inviting warmth of the evening.
