## Excerpt

ESPRIT C. KEI GINZA by A.N.D., NOMURA Co., Ltd. is a three-story fine dining establishment in Tokyo, blending traditional Japanese materials with a modern modular design.

## Editorial

Ginza has always been a place where Japanese craftsmanship meets an international taste for refinement. At ESPRIT C. This connection is evident through Chef Kei Kobayashi’s collaboration with Toraya, merging culinary innovation with a tradition focused on precision, seasonality, and the art of hand-making.
Designed by A.N.D., NOMURA Co., Ltd., the three-story restaurant embraces the process of creation. Cooking is not hidden from diners; preparation, service, and the transformation of ingredients are part of the overall experience. The architecture guides guests from the bustling street into a space where anticipation builds through sequences, partial views, and different levels of enclosure. This progression reflects Japanese spatial culture. Rooms are revealed gradually, allowing each movement to be appreciated. Dark thresholds, turns in circulation, and controlled sightlines create a rhythm that aligns more with the unfolding of a meal than with traditional restaurant layouts. By the time guests reach the main dining area, they have already transitioned from the outside world.
A distinctive feature of the restaurant is the ceiling. An ichimatsu grid, showcasing a traditional Japanese checkerboard pattern, starts with recognizable order and then transforms into an array of suspended cypress and acrylic rods. This change from geometry to volume shifts based on perspective, lighting, and positioning within the room. This transformation has cultural significance. While ichimatsu holds historical meaning, its new interpretation connects it to the present. The combination of timber and acrylic allows natural textures and modern precision to coexist, creating a dialogue between past and present.
Materiality plays an important role. Cypress wood adds scent and texture, Jindai cedar reflects the darker character of timber preserved for centuries underground, and Date Kanmuri stone brings geological weight to the interior. Glass, resin, and silver leaf catch and scatter light across surfaces, evoking the movement of water through reflection. Toraya's presence is felt through its approach to craftsmanship rather than recognizable dessert motifs. The company’s long history is expressed through themes of repetition, material refinement, and the continuation of traditional knowledge using contemporary techniques. This partnership aligns with Kei Kobayashi’s culinary practice, where precision and experimentation are part of the same process.
The open kitchen gives a human scale to this experience. Chefs are visible across the counter, integrating their hands, tools, and ingredients into the restaurant's atmosphere. Counter dining brings guests closer to the preparation, while tables further back offer a more intimate setting. Both arrangements remain connected to the kitchen and the design overhead, ensuring that the creative process is present throughout the meal.
A.N.D., NOMURA Co., Ltd. applies a high level of precision to details often overlooked. Foot bars are shaped for comfort during longer meals, working surfaces blend into the overall design, and lighting is thoughtfully positioned to highlight food, crystal, timber, and hands. Hospitality is expressed through these small details as well as through larger architectural elements.
The private dining room creates a different mood with warm tones, stone, timber, and reflective surfaces. It shares the same material palette while providing a greater sense of separation from the main dining area, extending the restaurant's narrative into a more intimate space. Environmental considerations are integral to the design. Standardized timber modules reduce fabrication waste, locally sourced timber cuts transportation distances, and existing Date Kanmuri stone is preserved and reimagined. The open kitchen also features a ventilation system that lowers exhaust volume by 17%, linking efficiency with the visible process of preparation.
 
ESPRIT C. KEI GINZA brings together cuisine, craftsmanship, and contemporary Japanese design in a cohesive way. Kei Kobayashi, Toraya, and A.N.D., NOMURA Co., Ltd. engage with tradition, reshaping and advancing it. Through the elements of timber, stone, light, and the movement of the kitchen, the restaurant embodies an ongoing act of creation in its architectural form.
